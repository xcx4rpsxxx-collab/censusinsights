Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

CensusInsights does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No data collection. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • No network access. All maps and census data are included in the app and stay on your device. The app does not connect to the internet. Tapping a source link opens that website in Safari, which is governed by that site's own privacy policy.
  • On-device settings. The app remembers the last map view you looked at and any map colors you choose. These are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, the app creates an image of the current map and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice; the app does not see or keep it.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions: see the support page.
