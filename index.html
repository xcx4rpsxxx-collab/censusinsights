CensusInsights100 years of America, mapped

A century of who lives where in the United States

CensusInsights is an iPhone and iPad atlas of U.S. census data. Slide from 1920 to 2020 and watch every county change, then zoom in to neighborhoods for income, rent, commuting and more.

  • History: race and ethnicity by county for every census from 1920 to 2020, with census tracts from 1990.
  • Today: 26 American Community Survey measures, by state, county and census tract.
  • Yours: change any map color, or pick a preset, including a colorblind-safe one.
  • Private: everything is on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection needed.

This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.
